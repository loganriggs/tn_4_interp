Additional Pruning Sweep on Fine-tuned Sparse Model
====================================================
Starting from: 84.3% accuracy, 10% dense (20,855 / 208,540 nonzero)

Question: how much further can we prune the already-sparse model?

Key observations:
- Global and per-layer give nearly identical results
- Accuracy is robust up to 25th pctile (7.5% dense): 80.8%
- Sharp cliff between 25th and 50th pctile: 80.8% -> 63.0%
- Below 50th pctile, degrades slowly to ~50% (still above chance=10%)
- The top 25% of nonzero weights carry almost all the accuracy

Layer distribution note:
- Layer 0 (47->60) dominates: 92% of all nonzero weights
- At any pruning level, L0 retains most weights, L2 retains fewest
- Global pruning naturally removes more from L0 (bigger layer)
  while per-layer pruning is proportional within each layer
- In practice, very similar results either way

Conclusion: the model has a clear "important core" of ~15k weights
(7.5% dense) that preserves 80%+ accuracy. Below that, accuracy
drops steeply. The remaining 5k weights (from 15k to 21k) provide
the final 3.5% accuracy boost from 80.8% to 84.3%.
