Key takeaway: expansion dominates lambda. At 4x you get 98% Hoyer
with ~zero fidelity cost. But this is somewhat trivially explained
by the zeros — the expanded cores embed the original in a much
larger (mostly-zero) tensor. The real test is expanding h in the
original L/D/R parametrization, where new hidden dims actually
need to learn useful decompositions.

Also added early stopping (patience=100, rtol=1e-4) to run_phase3
since everything converges by ~200-400 steps.
